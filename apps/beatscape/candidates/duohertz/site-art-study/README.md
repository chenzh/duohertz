# duohertz site art study — internal, unapproved

Run `node apps/beatscape/scripts/render-duohertz-site-art-study.mjs` from the repository root to rebuild with local Chromium. No network service is used.

The study uses the new site's ink `#111421`, cream `#fff9ef`, coral `#f34d65`, aqua `#67eee0`, and yellow `#ffda75`. The two endpoints and central frequency bend refer to one-key/two-key play, while the print dots and sharp diagonals relate to the candidate covers and page UI. All geometry is newly drawn here, without a Persona or other game asset.

- `mark-study.svg` and `og-study.svg` are editable vector studies.
- `icon-192-study.png`, `icon-512-study.png`, `apple-touch-180-study.png`, and `og-1200x630-study.png` are local renderings at the eventual site sizes. Their filenames intentionally differ from the release packager's required approved names.
- [Local comparison board](index.html) shows the mark at small sizes and the share card.
- `board-390-study.png` and `board-1280-study.png` are local screenshots of that board at mobile and desktop widths.

The English line is the goal document's **first-choice candidate**, not a final slogan decision. The share card visibly says `INTERNAL VISUAL STUDY · NOT RELEASED`. Before any release, review small-size recognition, contrast, text readability, western audience response, originality/rights, and consistency with the approved character/cover art. Then create separately approved production art and sign it with the current site source; this directory is not an approved `--site-art` input.
